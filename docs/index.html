About this PoC

This page is a lab for DNS delegation responses and TLS certificate handling. The local DNS checks compare referrals and A records returned by controlled authoritative servers; they do not test a recursive resolver or prove a live resolver defect. The TLS check reads the server's report for a certificate signed with a recovered eMudhra intermediate CA key.

The two buttons fetch HTTPS responses from separate hosts. The DNS check reports the server role reached; the TLS check displays the server's certificate report. These results do not prove that a two-stage attack succeeded on one connection. A failed fetch() does not expose the reason, so certificate rejection and connection failure cannot be distinguished.

The compromised intermediates have been revoked. The incident report says Firefox and Chrome block them from 17 October 2026, Microsoft platforms from 20 October, and Apple products from 22 October. Clients that do not use revocation information or Certificate Transparency may remain affected until updated. This check reports only this browser's connection and does not predict another client's result.
